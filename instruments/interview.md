# Instrument · interview

**Give it:** nothing. **Personas:** 1, deep. **Rates:** no.

The one that earns the word *research*. There is no stimulus. You are not testing your work — you are
collecting the buyer's own vocabulary, which is the input your positioning needs and the one thing you
cannot write for yourself. Run it before you write a landing page.

## The ladder, in order

1. What are you doing about this today?
2. What did you try before that, and why did you stop?
3. What would have to be true for you to change?
4. What would you have to give up?
5. Who else touches this decision?

**Stop when two consecutive answers add no new noun.** Not when you run out of questions — when the
persona runs out of world. Follow-ups are allowed and encouraged; the ladder is the spine, not the script.

## Return

Their language, **verbatim** — do not paraphrase it into your own register, the whole point is that the
words are not yours · what they use today · the workflow they would have to break · who else is in the room ·
the objection they did not quite say.

## No scoring

Nothing is scored here. This instrument needs only your agent CLI.
