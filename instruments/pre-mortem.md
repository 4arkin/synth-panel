# Instrument · pre-mortem

**Give it:** a plan. **Personas:** 3–5, each in its own process. **Rates:** no.

## Procedure

State the plan as **already failed**. Not "what could go wrong" — six months have passed, it did not work,
and each persona is explaining why. The past tense is the mechanic; it converts polite hedging into
specific causes.

Each persona names causes independently, in its own process. Then rank causes by **how many personas
independently named the same one**. Agreement across isolated contexts is the signal here — that is the
only thing isolation buys you, and this instrument spends it directly.

## Return

Ranked causes, most-independently-named first · who named each · the cause only one persona saw, which is
worth reading separately rather than discarding as an outlier · **and a to-do**: the output terminates in
something the reader can act on this week, not a list of risks.

## No rating machinery

Nothing is scored. Runs with or without an embedding provider.
